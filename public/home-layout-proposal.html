FAQ stays at the bottom

Why place the prompt grid before guides and trust content?

Because the homepage should behave like a library first. Most users arrive wanting to browse prompts, not read product explanations.

Why keep guides on the homepage at all?

Guides are still useful for SEO and for users who need more context. They just work better as a secondary recommendation block.

Why reduce the visual weight of About / Contact / Policies?

Those pages matter for trust, but they should support the main browsing flow rather than compete with it.
